# Chinese Work Vocabulary: RFID, device support, Shenzhen trip

> [!tip] Method
> 3 words/day. Hán-Việt helps: many technical terms map almost 1:1 to Vietnamese (e.g. 网络 võng lạc ↔ mạng lưới). Learn the Hán-Việt link first, then the pinyin tones.
> Pinyin and Hán-Việt are given to the best of my knowledge. Check tones with a dictionary app (Pleco) before relying on them.

## D. Ready-made phrases

|---|---|---|
| 多少钱？ | Duōshao qián? | How much? |
| 有现货吗？ | Yǒu xiànhuò ma? | Do you have it in stock? |
| 可以便宜一点吗？ | Kěyǐ piányi yìdiǎn ma? | Can it be a bit cheaper? |
